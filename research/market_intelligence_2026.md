### Notable New Entrant: Latch

Latch launched as an AI-first property management platform. Key differentiators:
- **AI Agent "Matt"**: Autonomously handles rent chasing, document analysis, compliance reminders, and tenant communication. Landlords report saving **5–10 hours/week**.
- **Pricing**: Free tier (3 leases), Pro at **£20/month** (£192/yr). Effective cost ~£4/property/month at 5 properties.
- **MTD**: Built-in, automatic — not a bolt-on.
- **Open Banking**: Integrated for automated transaction matching.
- **Weakness**: Content marketing-heavy — many of their "comparison" articles rank their own product #1, suggesting they are still building credibility.

> **Strategic Note**: Latch is the most direct competitor to LandLordLens's roadmap. Their AI features are 12–18 months ahead of current LandLordLens capabilities. The differentiation must be **compliance depth** (RRA-specific, Section 8 wizard, PRS Database readiness) which Latch has not prioritised.

## 2026 Market Dynamics

### The MTD Forcing Function
Making Tax Digital for ITSA becomes mandatory in **April 2026** for landlords earning over £50k gross. This is a mass market forcing event — landlords on spreadsheets MUST switch to compliant software or face HMRC penalties. This is a significant customer acquisition window.

| Threshold | Date | Estimated Landlords Affected |
|-----------|------|------------------------------|
| £50,000+ gross income | April 2026 (now live) | ~450,000 |
| £30,000+ gross income | April 2027 | +Further ~350,000 |
| £20,000+ gross income | April 2028 | +Further ~500,000 |

### The Renters' Rights Act Forcing Function
Phase 1 of the RRA is live as of 1 May 2026. Every self-managing landlord now:
- Cannot issue Section 21 notices.
- Must navigate 37 Section 8 grounds with specific notice periods.
- Must maintain Ombudsman-quality records.
- Will need to register on the PRS Database (late 2026).

This creates urgent demand for compliance tooling specifically designed for the post-RRA world.

### AI Becoming Table Stakes
The emergence of Latch signals that AI-assisted property management is no longer a differentiator — it will become baseline expectation by 2027. LandLordLens should plan an AI roadmap (see gap analysis).
